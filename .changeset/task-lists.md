`<dsk-markdown-editor>` edits task lists (`- [ ]` / `- [x]`) as lists with boxes to tick — a click ticks a box, `[ ] ` or `[x] ` typed at the start of an item makes a task — instead of keeping them as a block of Markdown: `@dskripchenko/markdown-editor` 0.9.0 and `@dskripchenko/wysiwyg` 0.13.0.
